02How do you track and guarantee campaign results?
We don't deal in vanity likes or bot views. Every campaign is tracked through custom UTM links, exclusive discount codes, and platform analytics. You receive access to our dedicated OG Hub Client Portal, where you can inspect content drafts, monitor live milestones, and review comprehensive post-campaign ROI reports.
03What does OG Hub charge?
We offer transparent, fixed sprint pricing and monthly retainers based on your campaign scope and creator tier. Unlike traditional agencies that mark up creator compensation by 50–100% in the dark, we operate with complete financial transparency so you know exactly where your budget goes.
04Why hire OG Hub instead of reaching out to creators in-house?
Creator outreach has an average 5% response rate when done cold. Following up with 100 creators, negotiating rates, managing product dispatches, and reviewing draft scripts takes hundreds of hours. OG Hub has direct relationships with top creators and managers, locking in preferred rates, ironclad contracts, and on-time deliverables while your team stays focused on product and fulfillment.

15–20 minutes direct with Ashutosh Mishra. We review your current revenue numbers, your target audience, and your margins to determine which creator tier and campaign angle will deliver the highest ROI. No high-pressure sales pitch—just actionable clarity.
